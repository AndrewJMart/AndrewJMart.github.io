# A Long Time Coming....

Over the last couple of months, I realized how far a village and a vision can get you. Through copious studying and reassurance from friends, family, and loved ones I landed a position I never thought possible. This post is a reminder to myself and you that anything you set your mind to is truly possible. Even if you fail in the pursit of your goals, you will be in a better place than you currently are. I want to give a special thank you to those involved in my success.

# A Long Way To Go....

On the flip side, this adventure has also caused me to reflect on my life. The primary discovery of my self reflection is despite my newfound success story, my journey has just begun. Enduring this whirlwind has made me realize how important it is to not let your environment consume you. As prior mentioned, you are the summation of who you surround yourself with so if in the pursuit of your dreams you neglect those around you... you in turn are nothing. While I am forever grateful for my new opportunity, it has caused me to realize other shortcomings in my mental health. Particularly, upon receiving the job my mind immediately shifted to what's next. I have been at the job for two months and my mind has only focused on what's next. There is value in pursing the next step, but I have not been admiring where I am at currently. If you told my freshman, business major self at Cal Poly I would be a SWE @ Apple after graduating I would not believe you. I want to thank those around me for the support I received and also commit to bettering myself for my sanity along with yours.
